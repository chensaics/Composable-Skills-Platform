# GEO Research: Princeton Study & Optimization Methods

## Paper Overview

**Title:** GEO: Generative Engine Optimization
**Authors:** Princeton University, IIT Delhi, Georgia Tech, Allen Institute for AI
**Published:** November 2023 (arXiv:2311.09735), Accepted KDD 2024
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735

## Core Insight

**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** is to AI search what SEO is to traditional search.

Unlike traditional SEO (ranking in search results), GEO focuses on **being cited** by AI systems. AI search engines don't rank pages — they cite sources. Being cited is the new "ranking #1".

## The 9 Optimization Methods

| # | Method | Visibility Boost | How to Apply |
|---|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Cite Sources** | +40% | Add authoritative citations and references to content |
| 2 | **Statistics Addition** | +37% | Include specific numbers, data points, quantitative info |
| 3 | **Quotation Addition** | +30% | Add expert quotes with proper attribution |
| 4 | **Authoritative Tone** | +25% | Write with confidence and expertise |
| 5 | **Easy-to-Understand** | +20% | Simplify complex concepts for broader accessibility |
| 6 | **Technical Terms** | +18% | Include domain-specific terminology appropriately |
| 7 | **Unique Words** | +15% | Increase vocabulary diversity, use distinctive phrasing |
| 8 | **Fluency Optimization** | +15-30% | Improve readability, flow, grammatical quality |
| 9 | **Keyword Stuffing** | **-10%** | ⚠️ AVOID — actively decreases AI visibility |

## Best Combinations

| Combination | Effectiveness |
|-------------|--------------|
| **Fluency + Statistics** | Highest overall boost |
| **Citations + Authoritative Tone** | Best for professional content |
| **Easy Language + Statistics** | Best for consumer content |
| **Technical Terms + Citations** | Best for academic/scientific content |

## Domain-Specific Recommendations

| Domain | Best Methods | Avoid |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Technology | Technical Terms, Citations, Statistics | Oversimplification |
| Business/Finance | Statistics, Authoritative Tone, Citations | Vague claims |
| Healthcare | Easy Language, Statistics, Citations | Jargon overload |
| Legal | Citations, Quotations, Authoritative Tone | Informal language |
| Education | Easy Language, Examples, Structure | Complexity |
| E-commerce | Statistics, Social Proof, Clear Benefits | Feature dumps |

## GEO Content Structure Checklist

- [ ] "答案优先"格式 — 直接回答放在顶部
- [ ] H1 > H2 > H3 清晰层级
- [ ] 2-3 句短段落
- [ ] 对比数据用表格
- [ ] 要点用列表（bullet points）
- [ ] 至少 2 个权威引用
- [ ] 至少 3 个具体数据点
- [ ] 至少 1 个专家引言
- [ ] 词汇多样，无重复表述
- [ ] 无关键词堆砌

## GEO vs Traditional SEO

| 维度 | Traditional SEO | GEO |
|------|----------------|-----|
| **目标** | 在搜索结果页排名前 10 | 被 AI 答案引用 |
| **测量** | SERP 排名、点击率 | 被引用次数、可见性分数 |
| **关键因子** | 外链、关键词、Core Web Vitals | 结构化数据、权威引用、时效性 |
| **Schema** | 提升 Rich Results | 提升 AI 引用率（FAQPage +40%） |
| **关键词策略** | 匹配搜索查询 | 匹配回答格式 |
| **更新频率** | 3-6 个月 | 30 天（3.2x 引用率提升） |
